A/B Testing Required — The 45% retention rate is sourced from Harvard Business Review (p.22, "Marketing winning back lost customers") for a 6-month / 20% discount win-back strategy. This assumption must be validated in production via A/B testing. The model's cost savings are highly sensitive to the actual retention rate (see chart above): at 45% retention the model is profitable at almost every threshold, but at 15% retention only ~53% of RF thresholds (30% for LightGBM) beat the no-model baseline, and savings are marginal.
